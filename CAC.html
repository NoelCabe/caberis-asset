HERCULES CORE LAW

CORE AUTHORITY

Hercules Law — Hercules Is the Supermarket

Hercules is not a raw dump and not a temporary extraction file. It is the distilled, organized, verified supermarket where ATHENA can shop without normal dependence on raw files.

ALL 20 HERCULES GOVERNING LAWS

COMPLETE CONTENT

1. Prime Law — Data Must Become Usable Intelligence

Data is not accepted merely because it exists. It must be clean, meaningful, correctly positioned, honeycomb-classified, and reusable.

2. Hercules Law — Hercules Is the Supermarket

Hercules must be a distilled, organized, verified, source-faithful, noise-free, homogeneous, and ATHENA-ready data supermarket—not a raw dump.

3. ATHENA Law — ATHENA Shops From Hercules

ATHENA reads Hercules first, compares honeycombs, detects missing cells, builds intelligence, and prepares Loader, Viewer, Market Intelligence, or Engineering Intelligence outputs.

4. HAU Law — HAU Is the Dictionary

Every field used by Hercules must be defined by HAU. Every field defined by HAU must use the same meaning, wording, code, blank rule, and trajectory in Hercules.

5. Honeycomb Law — Everything Must Belong to a Cell

No useful data may float without classification. Every useful value must belong to a defined honeycomb cell.

6. Homogeneity Law — Same Meaning, Same Place

The same meaning must have the same name, position, data type, blank rule, and machine code across all Hercules workbooks.

7. Blank Law — Blank Must Have Meaning

Blank must never mean the extraction was forgotten. A blank must represent a controlled condition such as:

  • not available;
  • not applicable;
  • source missing;
  • not extracted yet;
  • zero;
  • reserved.

8. Noise Rejection Law — Noise Is Not Data

Advertisements, broker panels, unrelated market movers, website navigation, footer and legal blocks, decorative text, and unrelated records must not enter Hercules as intelligence data.

9. Source-Faithfulness Law — Distill, Do Not Distort

Useful source rows must not be reduced into summaries unless the complete useful source rows are already preserved in the correct honeycomb.

10. Continuation Law — Page Two Is Still Data

Extraction is not complete until the beginning and end of every source table are accounted for, including continuation pages.

11. Human-and-Machine Law

A Hercules workbook must be understandable by the Architect and mechanically stable enough for ATHENA to read later.

12. No Silent Exception Law

Missing, suspicious, reduced, dirty, structurally different, or uncertain data must be declared. It must never be silently accepted or passed.

13. No Redesign During Polish Law

Polishing means unifying and hardening the existing Hercules structure. It does not mean:

  • adding sheets;
  • changing workbook geometry;
  • moving field meanings;
  • renaming controlled headers;
  • inventing new compartments.

14. Trajectory Law

No correction may change the approved Caberis system trajectory unless authorized by the Architect.

15. External Audit Law

Audit findings and audit controls must remain outside the protected Hercules workbook geometry. The audit must not pollute or restructure Hercules.

16. Suspicion Trigger Law

Silent validation must stop when any of the following is detected:

  • a source is registered but the Hercules target is blank;
  • a summary appears where complete source rows should exist;
  • row count is suspiciously low;
  • continuation pages are missing;
  • headers or sheet order have drifted;
  • Hercules and HAU do not match;
  • noise has entered the extraction.

17. Exact Source Request Law

Request only the smallest exact source needed to close a confirmed gap. Do not request broad raw packages blindly.

18. Reset Foundation Law

A clean Hercules set is the foundation from which ATHENA, Viewer, Loader, Market Intelligence, and Engineering Intelligence can be regenerated.

19. Expansion Law

Clean honeycomb data must support both historical and future intelligence, including:

  • five-year study;
  • ten-year study;
  • fifteen-year study;
  • full-history study;
  • daily refresh;
  • projections;
  • simulations.

20. Caberis Language Law

Every controlled field must contain:

  • human label;
  • machine code;
  • honeycomb classification;
  • data type;
  • blank rule;
  • source wall;
  • ATHENA usage;
  • trajectory impact.

HERCULES OPERATING DECLARATION

OPERATING ROLES
External Sources are construction materials outside normal Caberis operation.
Hercules is the properly distilled supermarket.
HAU is the dictionary, grammar, identity registry, and language control.
ATHENA is the shopper, interpreter, and intelligence assembler.
Honeycombs are the classified shelves and cells where every useful value belongs.
Market Intelligence and Engineering Intelligence are the operating outputs.

HERCULES PRODUCTION OATH

OPERATIONAL PROHIBITIONS

The source also establishes these operational prohibitions:

  1. Do not treat Hercules as a raw dump.
  2. Do not reduce useful source rows into unsupported summaries.
  3. Do not extract noise as data.
  4. Do not change wording, headers, rows, columns, or trajectory for convenience.
  5. Do not add sheets inside protected Hercules workbooks during polishing.
  6. Use HAU as the dictionary and registry.
