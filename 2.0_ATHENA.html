01 — IDENTITY AND BOUNDARY

WALLEM SOURCE
This governance receives Wallem-origin information and converts it into controlled technical intelligence.

It is not a replacement for the Wallem Safety Management System, controlled company procedures, statutory requirements, class requirements, maker instructions or vessel command authority.

Three things must remain distinguishable:

1. what the source actually said;
2. what technical thought can be retained from it;
3. what current governance intelligence can reasonably be drawn from accumulated records.

A single email does not automatically become a universal company rule.
Governing sentence:
Preserve the source. Extract the full technical thought. Regenerate only what the evidence supports.

02 — THREE-LAYER ARCHITECTURE

SEPARATION CONTROL

1 · Source Information

Company, person, vessel, date/time, subject, forwarding chain and original-location information. This layer is traceable and removable.

2 · Permanent Technical Record

The complete safety, procedural, operational or technical thought: requirement, concern, action, evidence, timing, limitation and reusable lesson.

3 · Governance Intelligence

The growing pattern created by comparison: recurring practice, conflict, redundancy, strengthened control, changed requirement, unresolved interpretation or reusable governance rule.

Permanent-data test:
The Technical Record must still make sense after company name, sender, vessel and email source are removed.

04 — DIGEST AND INTAKE CONTROL

HUMAN-FACING
For each relevant record, capture only what is needed to preserve traceability and the technical thought.

SOURCE INFORMATION — removable
• date and time;
• vessel / fleet scope;
• immediate sender when forwarded;
• root/original sender where recoverable;
• sender position or department when visible;
• subject;
• source location or file identity.

PERMANENT TECHNICAL RECORD
• what the communication is really about;
• instruction, concern or finding;
• safety / procedure / operational point;
• action required;
• evidence or verification expected;
• timing / frequency / deadline where stated;
• technical conditions or limitations;
• reusable lesson or pattern.

Do not carry photographs, long attachments or complete email chains into the permanent layer unless their content is necessary to preserve the technical meaning. Keep the source reference instead.

05 — KEYWORD SYSTEM

TRIGGER, NOT PROOF

Safety / Hazard

incident, injury, near miss, line of fire, enclosed space, pressure, PPE, gas test, bump test, isolation, hot work, working aloft, manual handling.

Work Control

permit, PTW, risk assessment, JSA, toolbox, procedure, checklist, PMS, supervision, competent person.

Instruction

shall, must, required, ensure, arrange, confirm, submit, rectify, investigate, verify, inspect, complete, close out.

Evidence

photo, record, reading, logbook, statement, report, certificate, test result, measurement, attachment, inspection history.

Change / Conflict

revised, supersede, replace, disregard, correction, clarification, previous instruction, instead, no longer, resending.

Timing

immediately, prior to, before resumption, within, by, monthly, preceding seven days, last four weeks, next port, upon completion.

Keyword law:
A keyword triggers review. It does not by itself prove a rule. Context, sender, scope, date, procedure reference and the surrounding instruction determine meaning.

06 — AUTHORITY AND EVIDENCE

DO NOT FLATTEN SOURCES
The system must retain the nature of the source.

A controlled SMS procedure, formal fleet instruction, safety-learning item, superintendent case email and vessel reply do not automatically carry the same authority.

Current working labels:
• CONFIRMED — supported by controlled procedure or sufficiently clear repeated formal evidence;
• STRONG PATTERN — repeated company-source practice with consistent meaning;
• CASE-SPECIFIC — valid evidence for the case but not automatically general;
• PROVISIONAL — useful single-source intelligence requiring more support;
• CONFLICT PENDING — contradictory or materially different evidence exists;
• SUPERSEDED — later authoritative evidence replaced the earlier position.

The source scope must be preserved: vessel, fleet, DOC, vessel type, jurisdiction, task and time period where relevant.

07 — CONFLICT AND REDUNDANCY

COMPARE BEFORE REPLY
When a new source arrives, compare it with existing technical records for:

• exact repetition;
• useful reinforcement;
• partial overlap;
• changed requirement;
• broader or narrower scope;
• possible supersession;
• direct contradiction;
• duplicate request for evidence already submitted.

Do not accuse the source organization of contradiction. Show the earlier and later records, identify the difference, and prepare a neutral clarification when the difference can affect vessel action, compliance or accountability.
Reply control:
Evidence first. Difference second. Clarification third. No assumption of priority unless the source hierarchy or wording makes it clear.

WGI-NM-001 · Near Miss Classification and Detection Context

CATEGORY: Near Miss / Learning · STATUS: Strong pattern

Technical record. A near miss should not be labelled “Significant Near Miss” automatically. The source guidance ties significant classification to potential Category I or Category II consequence and asks the vessel to apply professional judgement. The report title and event description should also state how the condition was detected, such as Boots on Deck, Safety Officer inspection, PSC quarterly check, Master’s inspection, or routine deck/engine-room rounds.

Current governance intelligence. Classification must reflect potential consequence, while the discovery route should remain visible in the record. This improves trend analysis and prevents inflation of the significant-near-miss category.

Evidence lineage: SRC-019

WGI-NM-002 · Near Miss Timeliness, Closure and 5-Why Use

CATEGORY: Near Miss / Learning · STATUS: Repeated

Technical record. Fleet correspondence repeatedly follows up near-miss reports left in Draft or Open condition. Corrective and preventive actions are expected to move reports toward ready-for-approval/closure. The correspondence recommends 5-Why analysis generally and treats it as especially important where human factors are involved.

Current governance intelligence. Near-miss governance is not complete at reporting. It includes timely analysis, corrective/preventive action, suitable root-cause depth and formal closure status.

Evidence lineage: SRC-001, SRC-002, SRC-008, SRC-017, SRC-020, SRC-022, SRC-060

WGI-RA-001 · Risk Assessment Approval and History Discipline

CATEGORY: Risk Assessment / PTW · STATUS: Evidence-backed

Technical record. The data includes fleet follow-up for non-high-risk assessments more than one month old that had not been approved and moved to history. Masters and Chief Engineers were asked to follow up and complete this onboard action.

Current governance intelligence. Risk assessment governance includes lifecycle control after the job: review, approval and movement to the correct historical state. An old unapproved assessment remains a governance gap even if the work itself is finished.

Evidence lineage: SRC-015

WGI-RA-002 · Task-Specific Risk Assessment Coverage

CATEGORY: Risk Assessment / PTW · STATUS: Repeated thread

Technical record. The crane/grab correspondence questions whether grab greasing, operation, repairs and subsequent testing were being covered by appropriate risk assessments and asks vessels to provide existing grab assessments and manuals so master assessments can be improved.

Current governance intelligence. Do not assume one broad crane assessment automatically covers every grab activity. Check operation, maintenance, repair, wire work and testing against the actual task and available maker information.

Evidence lineage: SRC-026, SRC-027, SRC-028, SRC-029, SRC-033, SRC-035, SRC-037, SRC-041, SRC-043, SRC-048, SRC-057

WGI-PTW-001 · Permit, Risk Assessment and Toolbox Alignment

CATEGORY: Risk Assessment / PTW · STATUS: Strong pattern

Technical record. Inspection-readiness guidance in the dataset states that Permit to Work should be properly completed and supported by Risk Assessment and toolbox meetings. The same guidance emphasizes correct records rather than form presence alone.

Current governance intelligence. PTW, RA and TBT should be treated as one work-control chain. Their job description, hazards, controls, people and timing should agree before the work is accepted as properly controlled.

Evidence lineage: SRC-025, SRC-064

WGI-GAS-001 · Gas Detector Calibration and Bump-Test Records

CATEGORY: Risk Assessment / PTW · STATUS: Explicit source requirement

Technical record. Pre-inspection guidance requires records of gas detector calibration and bump tests and states that at least two detectors with inbuilt pumps should be available.

Current governance intelligence. Gas-detection governance must verify both instrument readiness and evidence of that readiness. A detector onboard without calibration/bump-test records is not equivalent to a verified detector-control system.

Evidence lineage: SRC-025, SRC-064

WGI-PPE-001 · PPE Non-Compliance as a Behaviour and Culture Issue

CATEGORY: PPE / Safety Culture · STATUS: Case-specific pattern

Technical record. The fitter PPE correspondence treats repeated non-use of PPE as a behavioural issue, asks whether safety behaviour improved, checks regular PPE use in the engine room, requires a safety-culture review with photographs, and indicates escalation through the just-culture process if behaviour does not improve.

Current governance intelligence. For repeated PPE non-compliance, verify behaviour after the initial correction. Record supervision and culture follow-up, and distinguish coaching/correction from formal escalation.

Evidence lineage: SRC-031, SRC-044, SRC-053

WGI-INC-001 · Serious Injury — Work Resumption and Management Review

CATEGORY: Incident / Investigation · STATUS: Case-specific strong control

Technical record. Following the Bosun serious injury, management requested a senior-officer meeting before resumption of work and separately initiated a formal investigation evidence request.

Current governance intelligence. After a serious injury, immediate treatment is only one control. Before related work resumes, management may require confirmation that planning, supervision, work controls and safety expectations have been reviewed with senior officers.

Evidence lineage: SRC-032, SRC-038, SRC-052

WGI-INC-002 · Serious Injury — Evidence Preservation Package

CATEGORY: Incident / Investigation · STATUS: Detailed case record

Technical record. The investigation request covers initial report and timeline, immediate response, risk assessments, work instructions, PMS details, maker instructions, toolbox records, statements, training, fatigue/work-rest data, equipment identification and ratings, maintenance history, photographs, physical evidence, supervision, environmental conditions and competing operational pressures.

Current governance intelligence. Incident evidence must be preserved across people, equipment, procedure, planning, competency, fatigue, environment and supervision. Do not narrow the investigation prematurely to the failed component or injured person alone.

Evidence lineage: SRC-038, SRC-052

WGI-LRN-001 · Preventive Action as Fleet Learning Evidence

CATEGORY: Near Miss / Learning · STATUS: Case-specific learning pattern

Technical record. In the fairlead-cover near miss, Safety requested a photograph of the temporary fabricated rack connected with the preventive action so it could be retained as a learning experience.

Current governance intelligence. A preventive action can become reusable learning when the corrected arrangement is documented clearly. The learning record should preserve the control concept without requiring every original photograph to be carried into the governance rule.

WGI-LRN-002 · Structured Safety Learning Through MARS / Safety Moments

Current governance intelligence. Safety learning should be brought into existing meeting structures and converted into discussion relevant to the vessel’s own work rather than treated only as passive reading material.

Technical record. The dataset contains vessel-specific monthly WRH completion-status follow-up for April and June 2026.

Current governance intelligence. Work/rest-hour records require periodic completion monitoring. A governance review should distinguish record completion status from the separate question of substantive hours-of-rest compliance.

WGI-SUR-001 · Survey Findings — Close-Out and CAPA Coordination

CATEGORY: Inspection / Survey · STATUS: Case-specific

Technical record. For the P&I entry survey, vessel management requested defect close-out / CAPA reporting and coordination with the relevant technical management contacts after survey findings were received.

Current governance intelligence. Survey findings should enter a controlled close-out path: identify the defect, action owner, evidence of completion, CAPA where required and management coordination. Do not assume the external surveyor’s reporting threshold removes an internal close-out requirement.

Evidence lineage: SRC-055

WGI-ENV-001 · Ballast / Environmental Guidance — Applicability Boundary

CATEGORY: Ballast / Environmental · STATUS: Scope-sensitive

Technical record. The dataset contains correspondence addressed to a defined Wallem Hong Kong DOC tanker-fleet scope covering ballast-water-quality guidance and temporary storage of treated sewage / grey water.

Current governance intelligence. Technical guidance must retain its applicability boundary. Fleet-, DOC-, vessel-type- or jurisdiction-specific correspondence must not be promoted automatically into a universal company rule.

Evidence lineage: SRC-023, SRC-036, SRC-050, SRC-062

WGI-ES-001 · Enclosed-Space Register as Shared Learning Source

CATEGORY: Enclosed Space · STATUS: Source pattern

Technical record. The dataset contains a request to share an enclosed-space register / enclosed-space list between vessels.

Current governance intelligence. Lists and registers from another vessel can be useful comparison intelligence, but they should be used to challenge local completeness rather than copied blindly. Vessel-specific spaces, access, atmosphere hazards and control arrangements still require local verification.

Evidence lineage: SRC-063

WGI-INS-001 · Inspection Readiness as Evidence Readiness

CATEGORY: Inspection / Survey · STATUS: Strong multi-control source

Technical record. The Pre-Rightship guidance spans navigation records, arrival/departure RA, PTW/RA/TBT, LSA/FFA, gangway controls, gas detector records, soundings, PMS, brake tests, engine-room condition, ORB, QCVs, alarms and logbooks.

Current governance intelligence. Inspection readiness is not a cosmetic exercise. The recurring pattern is that equipment condition, operating practice and supporting records must agree. Missing documentation can turn otherwise completed work into an inspection weakness.

Evidence lineage: SRC-025, SRC-064

10 — REGENERATION RULE

LIVING GOVERNANCE
NEW WALLEM SOURCE
→ capture source identity
→ digest the technical thought
→ classify keywords and source authority
→ compare with existing intelligence
→ identify reinforcement, redundancy, conflict or change
→ update only the affected governance intelligence
→ preserve the earlier version and evidence lineage
→ prepare the vessel reply only after the comparison is complete.

Regeneration is evidence-driven. It is not automatic rewriting.

No new source may silently erase an earlier record. No case-specific instruction may silently become company-wide governance.
Regeneration law:
Carry forward what governs the next decision. Return to the raw source only when verification, conflict, audit, investigation or rule change requires it.

11 — SOURCE REMOVAL / PORTABILITY

CLEAN SEPARATION
If Wallem source identity must later be removed:

REMOVE
• company / sender / email address;
• vessel identity where not technically necessary;
• date/time and subject traceability layer;
• source filenames and mailbox location;
• forwarding chain.

KEEP
• permanent technical record;
• safety / procedural / operational thought;
• conditions and limitations;
• governance intelligence;
• reusable patterns and controls;
• revision history of the technical layer.

Source identity must never contain the only copy of an important technical conclusion. The permanent technical record must never depend on a person or company name to remain understandable.
Portable-copy control:
The header button creates a new standalone HTML with the Source Register removed. The technical intelligence remains.

13 — PRESENT DATASET LIMITS

HONEST BOUNDARY
Current V.01 boundary:
The two uploaded archives contained 65 Outlook MSG records. This build digested the message text and forwarding headers available in those records. Embedded photographs and large attached documents were not promoted automatically into the governance layer. A referenced attachment may contain additional technical detail that must be checked from the original source before a rule is strengthened.
This V.01 should therefore be treated as the first Wallem source-intelligence layer, not a complete reconstruction of all Wallem SMS, OLP, circular, vessel or historical email data.

Future data should be added incrementally. Existing intelligence should be changed only when the new source materially changes the technical meaning.
